# Bird Fight 3 v1.2.2 — Arenas & Avian Polish

This update expands stage selection, rebuilds several arenas, brings six of the
original fighters up to the game's newer visual standard, and adds a polished
official trailer presentation.

## Stage variants and selection

- Map Select now separates Main stages and Variants into dedicated tabs, each
  with its own Random option, plus a Random All choice that can select from the
  complete stage catalog.
- Variant choices preserve their authored base stage in local matches, online
  matches, and replays.
- Redesigned Titan Dock, Parliament Towers, Null Roc Ascending, Void Crown,
  and the other boss-stage variants so they have distinct layouts and
  silhouettes instead of closely repeating their original arenas.
- Ashfall Rebirth no longer creates a full-width ceiling that can trap fighters
  above the playable arena.
- Integrated the new arena variants into their matching missions throughout
  The Still Sky campaign.

## City and Parliament Towers

- Rebuilt City as a series of broad rectangular skyscraper rooftops, with every
  smaller combat platform visibly attached to a building.
- Improved the City's skyline depth, moon placement, rooftop details, windows,
  and neon signs.
- Reimagined Parliament Towers as an extreme high-altitude rooftop battle with
  distant city layers, tower peaks, atmospheric depth, and clearer neon signs.
- Repositioned Parliament vents and rooftop details so they no longer cover the
  stage's sign text.

## Fighter presentation pass

- Polished the vector art, proportions, poses, animations, and skin handling for
  Pigeon, Eagle, Falcon, Phoenix, Hummingbird, and Turkey.
- Pigeon now keeps both legs visible while flying.
- Phoenix now raises and turns its head and gaze with its movement animations.
- Turkey's neck stays behind its head during vertical movement instead of
  stretching through the beak.
- Turkey's Panic Flap now visibly opens both wings, reaches a full spread, and
  closes them again before the move ends.
- Added semantic visual-geometry safeguards across base birds, skins, poses,
  and facing directions. Automated checks now catch eyes leaving the head,
  beaks obstructing eyes, broken mirrored proportions, missing limbs, and
  pose-specific animation regressions.

## Multiplayer presentation

- Network character select now matches the layout and presentation of the main
  roster screen more closely.
- Echo fighters display the icon of their base bird so their relationship is
  clear during online character selection.

## Official trailer

- Added a completely new 1080p official trailer presentation built from real
  gameplay, story scenes, boss encounters, stages, skins, and the full roster.
- Replaced broken staged and slow-motion shots with clean gameplay and authored
  in-engine presentation scenes.
- Rebuilt the opening around a silent pause, title reveal, and synchronized
  music-and-action impact.
- Aligned the complete roster into a balanced 8–7–7 layout and protected the
  heading and subtitle from card overlap.
- Added an offscreen export path so trailer footage can be rendered without
  opening or controlling the game window.

## Validation

- 550 automated tests pass with zero failures.
- Stage variants, story-stage routing, network roster presentation, fighter
  geometry, animations, skins, and trailer storyboard layout all have targeted
  regression coverage.
- The final trailer was visually reviewed and its full video/audio streams were
  decoded successfully.
