# Bird Fight 3 v1.5.0 — Final Flight

Bird Fight 3 reaches feature-complete status with its definitive combat,
interface, progression, and campaign release. This update gives every fighter
an authored competitive identity, rebuilds the front-end journey, adds major
rules and tournament systems, and brings The Still Sky to its full coalition
finale.

The project now enters maintenance mode. Future releases may still fix bugs or
make deliberately chosen improvements, but v1.5.0 completes the planned game.

## Complete roster combat

- Finished distinct normal movesets and combat identities for all 22 playable
  birds.
- Added deterministic authored frame data across the roster, including startup,
  active, recovery, cancel, launch, and clash behavior.
- Polished universal walking, dashing, crouching, short hops, aerial drift,
  shielding, perfect shields, dodges, grabs, throws, ledges, recovery, teching,
  missed-tech getups, stale moves, and attack clashes.
- Added richer training telemetry and move-guide coverage for the completed
  systems.
- Reworked CPU recovery, edge guarding, launch survival, and stage navigation
  without changing the deterministic simulation contract.

## Modes and rules

- Added a complete versus rules editor with saved presets, Smash and Stamina
  modes, stage selection, hazards, teams, items, and match modifiers.
- Added full Squad Strike and rebuilt local Tournament mode with configurable
  entrants, seeds, fighters, skins, CPU levels, and resumable brackets.
- Added the first-launch Training Academy and a redesigned in-match pause guide.
- Unified fighter records, unlock guidance, match history, replays, soundtrack,
  endings, and galleries inside the Vault.
- Synchronized multiplayer lobby rules and host-authoritative tuning for LAN
  and direct internet matches.

## Interface and presentation

- Redesigned the title screens, main hub, Fight hub, Adventure hub, Games &
  More, Vault, settings, profiles, fighter select, stage select, pause, results,
  rewards, and network screens.
- Added input-aware prompts throughout keyboard, controller, and Wii Remote
  flows.
- Added original Bird Fight title art, destination illustrations, animated hub
  previews, clearer match HUD hierarchy, centered player tags, and cinematic
  boss displays.
- Improved fullscreen-safe scene transitions and dialog layout across the
  application.

## Progression and The Still Sky

- Rebalanced fresh-profile starter content and made rewards, unlock receipts,
  Bird Coin transactions, and CPU attribution safer and clearer.
- Added the complete Easy, Normal, and Hard Adventure balance harness across
  all 40 missions.
- Fixed story completion, results, held-input transitions, objective routing,
  enemy-wipe progression, chase progression, scaled interaction geometry, and
  campaign platform climbing.
- Rebuilt the Null Rock finale as a multi-stage coalition invasion with a
  cinematic boss HUD, relay encounters, counterpart assaults, reinforcements,
  and a complete ending path.

## Audio and stability

- Expanded combat audio with original light, medium, and heavy impacts, attack
  swings, launch tails, shield block, parry, break, and clash sounds.
- Fixed the fullscreen Shop purchase freeze, oversized Bat ceiling freeze, and
  repeated gameplay-frame failure loop.
- Improved moving-platform support for fighters and Bat ceiling hang.
- Hardened targeting and HUD geometry for campaign battles with more than four
  fighters.
- Corrected the distribution dry run and added release-version consistency
  checks across Maven, the jar manifest, jpackage, tags, and release notes.

## Validation

- 1,530 regular automated tests pass with zero failures, errors, or skips.
- The jar-based distribution builds, exposes version 1.5.0, and completes its
  launcher dry run.
- The Windows app-image build completes and its packaged executable passes the
  release dry run.
- All 56 bundled audio files pass the embedded-hostname audit.
- The latest roster audit completed 19,404 matches with six draws/timeouts.
- The all-route Classic audit completed 9,856 combat attempts without a harness
  hang.
- The Adventure audit completed 2,880 attempts with zero harness cutoffs. Its
  AI pilot does not clear the final mission in the sampled runs; the owner
  separately playtested and accepted the finale's human difficulty.
- Fresh-profile, save/reload, tutorial, progression, Shop, replay, loopback LAN,
  network configuration, updater, fullscreen-scene, controller-mapping, and
  recent platform regressions all have automated coverage.

Hardware-dependent controller feel, two-computer networking, and the live
v1.4.0-to-v1.5.0 updater handoff remain practical player-environment checks;
their protocol, persistence, and updater components are covered by regression
tests.

## Install

Download `BirdFight3-1.5.0-win.zip`, extract it, and run
**Bird Fight 3.exe**. Java is included. Existing packaged installations can
also update through the in-game updater after this release is published.
