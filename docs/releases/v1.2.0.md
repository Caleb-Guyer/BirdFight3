# Bird Fight 3 v1.2.0 — The Still Sky

This is Bird Fight 3's largest update so far: a complete story-mode reboot,
a new fighter and stage, a cinematic finale, major campaign fixes, and a broad
presentation pass across the game.

## The Still Sky campaign

- A definitive 12-act campaign with 40 authored missions spanning the full
  world and the complete 21-bird story cast.
- Forced-character missions, story-driven choices between birds who are
  actually present, and full-roster selection for the finale replace the old
  unrestricted character-select flow.
- Elimination, survival, protection, activation, capture, escape, hold-zone,
  gauntlet, and multi-phase boss objectives use each arena's unique mechanics.
- Easy, Normal, and Hard campaign tuning, authored phase checkpoints, mission
  retry, Act Select, and direct mission replay within completed acts.
- Main Adventure, Tempest Run, and Episodes remain available through Legacy
  Stories. Existing profiles keep their old unlocks, rewards, and progress.
- Campaign progress uses stable story IDs and preserves completed missions,
  recruitment, seen scenes, difficulty, checkpoints, and first-clear rewards.

## A fully rebuilt presentation

- Dozens of animated in-engine cutscenes with camera movement, blocking,
  character animation, lighting, particles, fades, subtitles, sound effects,
  music continuity, manual/automatic text, pause, skip, and gallery replay.
- A new illustrated storybook prologue establishes the history of the Bird
  Fight world before Act 1.
- Dialogue has been rewritten as more direct, conversational speech and moved
  into `the-still-sky-dialogue.txt` so it is easy to revise.
- The ending now includes a large-scale Null Rock battle, Eagle and Pigeon's
  final conversation, a fully playable collapsing-cave escape using Pigeon's
  normal physics and moves, an illustrated epilogue, full credits, character
  photos, a thank-you message, and a post-credits scene.
- New campaign music covers the prologue, Crownlock Prison, defeat screen,
  Null Rock, farewell, cave escape, and credits. Full source information is in
  `CREDITS-AUDIO.md`.

## New fighter: Kiwi

- Kiwi joins the standalone roster as an easy-to-learn close-range fighter.
- His kit includes Rapid Probe, Burrow Charge, Spring Kick, Earth Stomp, and
  the Midnight Stampede ultimate.
- Kiwi is supported in standard fights, AI play, Classic, Training, tutorials,
  LAN/replay state, Bird Book pages, random selection, and the balance lab.
- Kiwi and Goose received a visual pass to match the roster's rounded bodies,
  oversized cartoon eyes, clean silhouettes, and readable gameplay-scale
  details.
- Kiwi is intentionally not part of The Still Sky's story continuity yet.

## New stage: Crownlock Prison

- Crownlock Prison is a full selectable arena and the setting for Blackout Key.
- The prison has a flat main floor, layered platforms, a breached ceiling,
  moving searchlights, prison architecture, and blast exits on the left,
  right, and ceiling.
- Stage levers release prisoners who charge across the arena and attack enemies
  in their path.
- Crownlock is unlocked through campaign progress or the relevant content pack
  instead of being available by default.
- A dedicated serious prison track replaces generic stage music.

## Gameplay, AI, and mission improvements

- Campaign enemies and bosses now remain defeated instead of silently
  respawning between objectives. This fix applies across Carrion Bargain,
  Carrion Audience, Perfect Weather, Blackout Key, and other multi-phase fights.
- Protection phases complete immediately when every threat has been defeated.
- Capture and hold objectives now require both horizontal and vertical
  proximity; flying above a point no longer captures it.
- Campaign allies can help with noncombat objectives after enemies are cleared,
  including Charles helping reset the Dead Air vents.
- Team missions end based on the surviving team rather than only the human
  player's life state.
- AI recovery now detects repeated bounce/stall loops and changes its recovery
  choice instead of cycling forever.
- Campaign AI timing, reaction windows, spacing, and punish behavior were
  adjusted to feel less mechanically perfect and more like a platform-fighter
  opponent.
- Knockback and hit reactions were strengthened to reduce low-knockback
  beatdown loops.
- Pelican can safely use Up Special into water.
- Charles's Raven-copy gameplay now performs the copied capture behavior
  correctly.

## Bespoke campaign battles and finale

- Falcon/Razorbill versus Eagle is now a dedicated cinematic 1v1 rather than a
  generic control-point match, with context-specific arena presentation and no
  unrelated Raven opponent.
- Null Rock is a gigantic background commander with authored phases, coalition
  assists, map interactions, a direct final duel, and protection from ordinary
  blast-zone deaths.
- The final assault uses the whole story roster without placing 21 simultaneous
  AI fighters in the simulation.
- Recorded Null dialogue is represented by Vulture's silhouette, preserving the
  copied flock-mind reveal.

## Interface, reliability, and progression

- Story hub, mission briefings, cutscene controls, results/failure screens, and
  credits layouts now scale and center correctly across window sizes.
- Campaign failure plays its own defeat music instead of the victory theme.
- Prologue, credits, and other special scenes unlock correctly in the gallery.
- Cutscenes reset presentation timing cleanly and include safeguards against
  frozen Back, Next, and Skip controls.
- Campaign objectives are clamped to playable arena bounds so required points
  cannot appear outside the map.
- Standard Fight, LAN, Classic, replay determinism, legacy progress, and player
  unlocks remain isolated from the campaign systems.

## Rewards

- Completing The Still Sky grants its completion achievement and 500 Bird Coins
  once.
- Campaign completion unlocks its finale rewards, including Beacon Crown and
  the Null Rock Vulture skin when needed.
- Crownlock Prison has its own campaign/content-pack unlock route.

## Validation

- 495 automated tests cover campaign content, progression migration,
  objectives, checkpoints, cutscene determinism, the finale escape, Crownlock
  Prison, fighters, balance systems, replay/LAN state, and existing game modes.
- The full release test suite passes with zero failures.

Install the Windows portable package, extract it, and run `Bird Fight 3.exe`.
Existing packaged installations can also update through the in-game updater.
