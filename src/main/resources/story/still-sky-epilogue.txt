# THE STILL SKY — EPILOGUE

## after_01|The First Unplanned Morning|FIRST_SKY
The wind returned without asking anyone where it belonged. It crossed the broken Crown platforms, rattled loose signs in the city, and sent every careful reconstruction chart sliding across Penguin's table. For the first time in years, a bad forecast felt like good news.

Pigeon did not become a king, a marshal, or the keeper of a master key. Pigeon spent the first morning carrying messages between regional councils, complained about every meeting, and still arrived early to the next one.

---

## after_02|A Lounge With More Doors|ELEVEN_LANDS
Charles rebuilt the lounge where three evacuation routes met. The new sign was smaller, the roof was stronger, and every chair faced another chair instead of a stage. Charles still performed confidence when the room went quiet, but now he admitted when his hands were shaking.

Old Sparrow's chair stayed near the window. Nobody turned it into a shrine. Birds sat beside it when they needed to remember that ordinary work—opening a gate, carrying a stranger, making room—could change the course of a world.

---

## after_03|Harbor Without Owners|OPEN_WING
Pelican organized rescue crews along Broken Harbor, then learned to ask before organizing everyone else. Goose painted the word OURS across the new breakwater and, after a long argument, added EVERYONE'S underneath it.

Razorbill wrote contracts that allowed a bird to walk away when an order endangered civilians. The first captain who complained received a very precise lecture and a bill for Razorbill's time.

---

## after_04|Fire, Ice, and Receipts|PERFECT_WEATHER
Phoenix helped relight the Ashfall thermals, this time with evacuation plans that did not depend on everyone surviving Phoenix's confidence. Penguin published every Crown design, every failure report, and every name that had been hidden in the archive.

Heisenbird signed the repair plans and the damage ledger with the same large handwriting. Recognition still mattered to him. He was finally learning that responsibility had to carry the same name.

---

## after_05|Roads That Wait|AGE_OF_WINGS
Roadrunner mapped routes between regions and, against every old instinct, stayed long enough to see travelers arrive. Hummingbird became the coalition's fastest courier and its most patient listener, although patience at Hummingbird speed still looked alarming to everyone else.

Tufted Titmouse found the narrow paths the Crown had ignored. Small communities stopped being blank spaces on a central map because Titmouse made sure they drew their own.

---

## after_06|Two Morning Bells|FIRST_FIGHT
Turkey and Rooster never stopped arguing. They simply learned which arguments improved a plan and which ones were performances for a crowd. Turkey coordinated ground shelters; Rooster trained rapid-response flocks. Each claimed the other copied the idea.

Their teams met at sunrise every month. One brought food, one brought noise, and both arrived prepared when the weather refused to cooperate.

---

## after_07|What the Dark Could Hear|WATCHED_FIGHTS
Bat returned below the map and listened until the caves sounded like stone again. When Bat heard something no one else could hear, Bat described it plainly and waited for questions instead of carrying the warning alone.

Shoebill opened the neutral crossings to inspectors from every region. Neutrality was no longer a locked door with Shoebill holding the key; it became a promise that the stronger side would not decide who counted.

---

## after_08|No Clean Pardons|BARGAINS
Vulture's scavenger flocks received the sanctuary Eagle had once made conditional on obedience. Vulture helped dismantle the remaining Crown relays under guard and answered publicly for the convoys he had attacked. Work was not forgiveness, and he did it anyway.

Opium Bird stopped treating prediction as permission. Grinch-Hawk returned stolen equipment whenever the reward exceeded the resale value—which happened more often after the council learned how to negotiate with him.

---

## after_09|Birds Who Stayed Difficult|BLACK_MIGRATIONS
Raven continued to trade information in directions nobody entirely trusted. The difference was that no single ruler could purchase all of it. Falcon trained regional guards to refuse unlawful orders and included Falcon's own record in the first lesson.

Eagle's final act remained in the history beside the lockdown, the drained regions, and Old Sparrow's death. The birds did not make him innocent to make his sacrifice meaningful. They remembered the whole bird.

---

## after_10|The Open Sky|STILLNESS
The Crown was gone. Natural winds ruined schedules, carried seeds into unexpected soil, and forced old enemies to share shelter. The world became less efficient and more alive.

Pelican, Goose, Phoenix, Penguin, Roadrunner, Hummingbird, Tufted Titmouse, Turkey, Rooster, Bat, Shoebill, Razorbill, Falcon, Vulture, Raven, Heisenbird, Opium Bird, Grinch-Hawk, Charles, and Pigeon did not become one perfect flock. They became many flocks that chose, again and again, to answer one another.

No ruler owned the wind. That was never the same as nobody being responsible for where it carried them next.
